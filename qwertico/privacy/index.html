Qwertico Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026 · Developer: Serhii Kibysh

Qwertico does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

All app preferences (such as sound settings or usage counters) are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted outside the app.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

If you purchase a subscription, payment and subscription management are handled by the store you bought it from (the App Store or Google Play). Qwertico has no access to your payment information.
